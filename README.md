# Homeostatic reinforcement learning on Crafter

A record and a formal theory of the Crafter organism experiments (27 Sep to 5 Oct 2026). It covers why the work started, what held and what failed, a theory that fits the results, and two extensions: homeostasis as a source of sample efficiency, and homeostasis and alignment.

| `PROVENANCE.md` | Check of each citation, with links and the supporting passage. |
| `REVIEW_LOG.md` | What each of five independent reviewers found in draft 1, and what changed. |
